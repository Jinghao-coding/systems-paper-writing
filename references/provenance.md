# Provenance

## Paper-analysis foundation

The [corpus](paper-corpus.md) records 28 publication versions from an analysis snapshot dated 2026-09-28. Its conference, agent, and journal notes identify passages and representative figures. Conference/journal editions of the same work are treated as related versions. This English edition reorganizes the recorded analysis into reusable writing guidance while preserving important conditions and version distinctions.

Public publication pages and DOI links are retained. Private editing artifacts, personal correspondence, local filesystem paths, manuscript diffs, and full paper PDFs are excluded from the public package. Historical personal editing experience informed the original skill; this version expresses reusable guidance and constructed examples independently of those private artifacts.

## Systems-writing guidance reviewed on 2026-10-05

- [Orchestra Research systems-paper-writing](https://github.com/Orchestra-Research/AI-research-SKILLs/blob/773a52944ba4747a18bd4ae9ade53fff041adcbc/20-ml-paper-writing/systems-paper-writing/SKILL.md), entry version 1.1.0, commit `773a52944ba4747a18bd4ae9ade53fff041adcbc`; entrypoint and `references/section-blueprints.md`.
- [Irene Zhang, Hints on how to write an SOSP paper](https://irenezhang.net/blog/2021/06/05/hints.html), 2021-06-05.
- [Roy Levin and David D. Redell, How (and How Not) to Write a Good Systems Paper](https://www.usenix.org/conferences/author-resources/how-and-how-not-write-good-systems-paper), originally published in 1983.

Adopted ideas include application/environment scope, reader prerequisites, claim–design–evidence correspondence, consequential alternatives, mechanism evaluation, and transferable lessons. The instructions and examples here are independently written. Fixed sentence counts, page allocations, contribution counts, obligatory first-page figures, and triple repetition of conclusions are replaced by task-dependent judgments. Venue policies and upstream exemplar claims are checked at their original sources when needed, not inherited as facts.
