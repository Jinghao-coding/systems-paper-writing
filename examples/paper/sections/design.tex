\section{Design}\label{sec:design}
Let $Q$ denote the waiting queue, $B=8$ its capacity, and $\hat{s}(r)$ the
estimated service time of request $r$. The estimate is available at arrival.
A dispatcher handles queue operations serially. The worker is non-preemptive.
The prototype uses one worker and one fixed model. The method does not claim
starvation freedom.

\begin{enumerate}
\item On arrival of $r$, if $|Q|\ge B$, reject $r$ and return an overload status.
\item Otherwise append $r$ to $Q$.
\item When the worker becomes idle, remove a request minimizing $\hat{s}(r)$
and execute it. Break ties by arrival order.
\end{enumerate}

The observation used to update $\hat{s}$ is added after completion. The update
schedule and initialization are not specified in this draft.

\begin{equation}\label{eq:latency}
L(r)=t_{\mathrm{done}}(r)-t_{\mathrm{arrival}}(r).
\end{equation}
This quantity is defined for completed requests.
