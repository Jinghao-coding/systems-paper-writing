\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}
A shared inference worker processes one request at a time. A burst can leave
short requests waiting behind long requests. GateQueue combines a queue budget
with shortest-estimated-service ordering. The contribution is an explicit
admission decision before ordering work on the worker. We claim lower tail
latency at equal service coverage.

The worker executes each admitted request to completion before selecting the
next request. This non-preemptive policy avoids discarding partial execution.
